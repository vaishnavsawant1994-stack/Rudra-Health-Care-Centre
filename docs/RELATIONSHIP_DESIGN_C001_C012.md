# Rudra relationship design previews C001–C012

This adds twelve linked dashboard previews covering doctor CRM, onboarding and verification, doctor profiles and visits, referral attribution, corporate accounts and quotations, corporate health camps, and partner directory, contracts and portal operations.

The screenshots supplied in the project conversation are treated as layout and content references. Preview rows, identities, contact details, account figures, dates and workflow states are synthetic examples. Images are fictional generated examples. The pages do not load patient records, verify professional credentials, send communications, create contracts, schedule visits or camps, or write operational records. Their controls are for preview and navigation only.

Protected previews live at `/relationships/design/c001` through `/relationships/design/c012`. Local-only previews live at `/design-preview/relationships/c001` through `/design-preview/relationships/c012`; the existing design-preview layout excludes these routes from production. The existing operational doctor, corporate and partner pages remain the entry points to the real workflows.

Pixel-level comparison against the supplied screenshots remains subject to browser review at the target display size.
