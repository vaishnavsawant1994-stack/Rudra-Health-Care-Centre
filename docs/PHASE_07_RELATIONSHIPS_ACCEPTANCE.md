## C010 and W020 route boundary

The screen inventory preserves C010's original target `/partners`, but that path is already the public W020 partner enquiry page. C010 is therefore source-mapped to `/relationships/partners`, protected by `partners.read`. The public page and `/partner` redirect were preserved. `/partner/portal` contains only a public unavailable notice and does not disclose directory contents.

## Cross-module and security boundaries

Doctor attribution uses existing lead, referral, appointment and task workflows. Summaries do not contain patient names or contact details. Corporate and partner lead activity remains account- and centre-scoped. Existing internal quote and finance contracts remain subject to their Phase 06 restrictions. Communications providers, referral incentives, special pricing, legal contracts, patient access for employers/partners and medical report access are not activated.
