## Cross-module and security boundaries

W007 public enquiries remain requests for staff review, not appointments. R013–R015 lead CRM contact attempts and drafts preserve separate operational contact permission and marketing consent. P013–P014 patient support/preferences do not grant access to protected account data. F013 support uses the existing internal task system. L014 report availability does not send notifications; no unverified Metropolis status can trigger a customer message. C001–C012 relationships do not authorize external messaging or expose clinical records. S004 consent remains authoritative. O006 tasks and O007 escalation rules are reused; they do not prove that an external contact was made.

Migration `0100_communications_governance_drafts.sql` adds encrypted, centre-scoped, draft-only provider policy and knowledge records plus append-only encrypted change snapshots. It has been exercised only in a disposable synthetic PostgreSQL schema and has not been applied to the persistent development database. The database remains at 0093; migrations 0094–0100 remain unapplied there. Manual-first Metropolis behavior is unchanged.
