# Rudra Phase 09 marketing previews M001–M011

Eleven marketing design previews cover the dashboard, channel health, content studio, asset library, editorial calendar, approvals, publishing monitor, social inbox, reviews, campaign builder, and audience / budget planning.

The screen references from the supplied images use a shared sample centre and fictional people, campaign records, review text, account states, metrics, and budgets. The campaign artwork at `public/images/marketing-health-awareness.webp` is generated illustrative content. Previews do not connect social accounts, contact anyone, approve medical content, authorize a budget, or publish posts. Real content calendar and campaign workflows remain available at `/marketing/content` and `/marketing/campaigns`.

Protected previews are available at `/marketing/design/m001` through `/marketing/design/m011`. Development-only previews are available at `/design-preview/marketing/m001` through `/design-preview/marketing/m011`; the design-preview layout prevents production access.

The references reproduce the supplied screen structure and sample content. Pixel-level approval still requires comparing rendered pages to the source images at their target viewport.
